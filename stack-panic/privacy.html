Privacy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Stack Panic is a browser game with no player accounts or game server. It stores game progress and preferences, such as scores, statistics, and sound preference, in your browser's local storage. You can remove this data by clearing this site's browser storage.

Advertising

Stack Panic uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve, personalize, measure, and protect advertising based on visits to this and other websites. Learn more in Google's advertising information. You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or opt out through aboutads.info.

External services

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you choose “Challenge a Friend,” your browser may share the score and challenge link using the sharing method you select. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.
